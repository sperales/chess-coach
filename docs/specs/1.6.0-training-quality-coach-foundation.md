## Product goal

Chess Coach must stop treating every engine detection as an exercise. It must identify a useful pedagogical opportunity, decide whether it is appropriate now, deliver it in a suitable format and retain evidence of learning.

The governing rule is `training quality > content quantity`.

## Release outcome

Version 1.6.0 answers: **Are we training the right thing, and can the player perceive that they are learning?**

The complete loop is:

```text
current analysis
→ Training Opportunity
→ Mastery + Recent Performance
→ Coach Decision
→ Training Composer
→ Flash / Scenario
→ Training Result
→ Mastery + Recent Performance + next review
```

## Priority and rollout

- All Must Have requirements are required for the release.
- Nova reactive visuals are Very Nice to Have and are attempted only after Must Have stability.
- Should Have work may move to a later release without weakening the foundation.
- Selection v2 ships in `shadow` mode by default. Legacy selection controls the player experience until explicitly configured as `active`.
- Historical records remain auditable. No destructive cleanup is allowed.

## Taxonomy v1

`TAX-001` The canonical concepts are:

1. `tactics_combinations` — Táctica y combinaciones.
2. `threats_defense` — Amenazas y defensa.
3. `king_safety_mate` — Seguridad del rey y mate.
4. `calculation_candidates` — Cálculo y candidatos.
5. `advantage_conversion` — Conversión de ventajas.
6. `simplification_exchanges` — Simplificación e intercambios.
7. `activity_coordination_initiative` — Actividad, coordinación e iniciativa.
8. `opening_development` — Apertura y desarrollo.
9. `pawn_structure_positional` — Estructura de peones y decisiones posicionales.
10. `endgames_technique` — Finales y técnica.

`TAX-002` Every opportunity has one primary concept, zero to two secondary concepts, confidence and structured evidence.

`TAX-003` Severity, phase, result, source side and move classification are attributes, not concepts.

`TAX-004` Mappings from existing Smart Tags and analysis signals are centralized and versioned. A primary concept requires confidence `>= 0.60`.

## Training Opportunity

`OPP-001` A Training Opportunity is the canonical pedagogical object between analysis and a delivered Flash or Scenario.

`OPP-002` It stores user ownership, canonical identity, objective, primary and secondary concepts, confidence, estimated difficulty, format eligibility, scoring components, publication state, algorithm versions and timestamps.

`OPP-003` One opportunity may reference multiple source games, analyses and move-analysis rows. Recurrence adds evidence instead of creating duplicate exercises.

`OPP-004` Existing `training_exercises` and `training_scenarios` remain delivery records and may link to an opportunity. They are not the canonical source of pedagogical truth.

`OPP-005` Opportunity states are `published`, `reserve`, `rejected`, `inactive` and `superseded`. A hard rejection is retained with its reason.

## Analysis currency

`CUR-001` For each game, the most recent valid completed analysis is current. Failed, cancelled, incomplete or superseded analyses are not current.

`CUR-002` When a current analysis changes, opportunities based on older analyses are revalidated without deleting their sources or prior scoring.

`CUR-003` A changed best move does not automatically invalidate an opportunity if the stored solution remains an accepted alternative and the pedagogical objective remains valid.

`CUR-004` If neither the solution nor an accepted alternative remains valid, the opportunity becomes inactive or superseded before it can be selected.

## Canonicalization and deduplication

`CAN-001` Exact canonical identity uses normalized FEN, side to move, primary concept, normalized objective, primary solution and accepted alternatives.

`CAN-002` FEN normalization preserves piece placement, side, castling and en-passant state while excluding move counters.

`CAN-003` Equivalent sources attach to one opportunity. Source history and recurrence are preserved.

`CAN-004` Basic similar-position detection may reduce selection priority or mark a redundant candidate. Embeddings and advanced semantic similarity are out of scope.

## Hard filters

`FIL-001` Hard filters are centralized, configurable, versioned and auditable.

`FIL-002` A candidate is rejected before scoring when any approved condition applies:

- analysis is not current;
- invalid FEN, move or solution line;
- incomplete or insufficient-confidence engine analysis;
- no primary concept with confidence `>= 0.60`;
- exact canonical duplicate already represented by a better opportunity;
- more than four equivalent alternatives without an evaluable objective;
- more than three meaningful player decisions or disproportionate depth;
- mate longer than six player moves;
- ordinary early opening without conceptual error, recurrence or meaningful deviation;
- the played move remains accepted and no additional teaching purpose exists;
- opponent opportunity has no relation to a relevant weakness, strength or Coach focus;
- extreme advantage with only a generic “maintain the advantage” objective.

`FIL-003` Each rejection stores a stable reason code, evidence and filter version. Logs must not duplicate the complete audit trail.

## Pedagogical Score v1

`SCORE-001` Hard-rejected candidates are not scored.

`SCORE-002` The versioned score is capped to `0..100`:

```text
relevance                 0..25
+ concept_confidence      0..15
+ decision_clarity        0..15
+ pedagogical_value       0..15
+ recurrence              0..10
+ adaptive_fit            0..10
+ novelty                  0..5
+ format_suitability       0..5
- ambiguity_penalty       0..20
- redundancy_penalty      0..25
- complexity_penalty      0..15
- overexposure_penalty    0..15
```

`SCORE-003` Severity contributes at most five points inside relevance and never determines quality alone.

`SCORE-004` Scores `>=65` publish, `50..64` enter reserve, and `<50` are rejected. Thresholds and components are persisted as calibratable v1 hypotheses.

`SCORE-005` Fixtures cover a low-CPL opponent move, an exact duplicate, deep mate, generic conversion from an extreme advantage and a valid defensive decision from a non-negative evaluation.

## Selection v2 and shadow mode

`SEL-001` Pedagogical Score answers whether content is useful. Selection Priority answers whether useful content belongs in today's plan.

`SEL-002` Selection Priority combines Pedagogical Score with due review, Coach focus alignment, recent-game relevance and failure recovery, minus fatigue and within-session similarity. Components, total, reason and evidence are persisted with an algorithm version.

`SEL-003` Structured reasons include `primary_focus_new`, `due_review`, `previous_failure`, `recent_game`, `mastery_consolidation`, `secondary_focus` and `maintenance`.

`SEL-004` Runtime modes are `legacy`, `shadow` and `active`; `disabled` is accepted as an alias of `legacy` for safe configuration compatibility.

`SEL-005` In shadow mode, legacy selection controls the session. Selection v2 evaluates the same eligible pool and stores a comparison without changing UI, order or delivery.

`SEL-006` Comparisons include selected identities, quality, concepts, difficulty, duplication, focus alignment, new/review mix and reasons.

## Format and difficulty

`FMT-001` One meaningful player decision that demonstrates the objective routes to Flash.

`FMT-002` Two or three meaningful player decisions that must sustain the objective route to Scenario.

`FMT-003` Content that cannot demonstrate its objective clearly in either format is not published. Severity never selects the format.

`DIF-001` Estimated difficulty is independent from severity and considers candidate count, alternative gap, concept, complexity, required line length and decision clarity.

`DIF-002` Observed difficulty uses attempts, highest hint, time to first move, total time, autonomy and result. No machine-learning model is introduced.

`DIF-003` Estimated and observed difficulty, inputs and algorithm versions remain auditable.

`DIF-004` Time to first legal submitted move is stored separately from total duration for Flash and Scenario. Speed alone never grants mastery.

## Mastery and Recent Performance

`MAS-001` Mastery is calculated per user and canonical concept with algorithm version, confidence and evidence counts.

`MAS-002` States and minimum requirements follow the approved addendum:

- `starting`: fewer than three valid opportunities or practice on one date;
- `learning`: at least three opportunities over two dates, with autonomy below 60% or frequent support;
- `consolidating`: at least six opportunities over three dates, seven-day window, adjusted autonomous resolution `>=65%`, and two reviews after at least three days;
- `stable`: at least ten opportunities over five dates, 21-day window, adjusted autonomous resolution `>=75%`, and two reviews after at least seven days without recurrent high-level hints.

`MAS-003` A single bad day does not destroy stable mastery. Persistent delayed-review failures, recurrent high hints or repeated serious game recurrence can reduce it.

`MAS-004` Inactivity does not erase history: after 30 days mark review pending, after 60 reduce confidence, and after 90 require confirmation before presenting the concept as currently stable.

`PERF-001` Recent Performance is separate and uses the latest five relevant opportunities or 30 days. States are `in_form`, `normal`, `attention` and `priority_review`.

`PERF-002` A stable concept with recent errors may receive temporary review without immediately losing mastery.

## Deferred review

`REP-001` The initial review model is deterministic and versioned, not a complete Anki algorithm.

`REP-002` Failure, multiple attempts, higher hints and excessive difficulty shorten the interval. Autonomous delayed success lengthens it.

`REP-003` The lifecycle is `new → practice → consolidation → occasional review`; every due date retains the rule version and reason.

## Coach Decision

`COACH-001` One persisted Coach Decision is the authoritative focus for Home, Training, Nova and generated objectives.

`COACH-002` Inputs are Player DNA, Recent Performance, Mastery, recurrence, confidence, training history, recent games and available published opportunities.

`COACH-003` Output contains primary focus, optional secondary focus, evidence, confidence, reason, session objective, creation time, reassessment conditions and algorithm version.

`COACH-004` Priority uses the approved model: recent severity `0..30`, recurrence `0..25`, mastery gap `0..20`, transfer failure `0..15`, trainability `0..10`, minus low confidence `0..20`, recent overexposure `0..15` and stable-without-issue `0..20`.

`COACH-005` The primary focus remains for at least three training days unless a new repeated serious signal appears. It is reassessed after three training days, ten new games or seven calendar days, whichever occurs first.

`COACH-006` A low-confidence concept cannot become primary without a repeated serious event. A stable concept cannot become primary without confirmed deterioration. A concept without quality opportunities cannot be selected.

## Training Composer

`COMP-001` Available plans are short (about five minutes/three activities), standard (eight to ten minutes/about five activities, default), and long (about fifteen minutes/about eight activities).

`COMP-002` The target mix is 60–70% primary focus, 20–30% secondary or consolidation, 10–20% maintenance, about 40% new and 60% review/recovery/consolidation.

`COMP-003` A standard plan normally contains three Flash and two Scenarios, adapted to actual quality and observed duration. At most one strong recent-game opportunity is included.

`COMP-004` A shorter high-quality plan is preferred to filling a quota with reserve, rejected, ambiguous or near-duplicate content.

`COMP-005` Within-session adaptation is limited to the approved deterministic rules: two consecutive difficult failures lower the next difficulty or replace a Scenario; three fast autonomous successes may increase difficulty slightly; hint level two or three schedules closer review.

## Completion, habit and goals

`END-001` Completion shows one compact panel with primary concept, completed activities, autonomy, mastery evidence/change, next review, secondary daily-goal/streak state and one evidence-based Nova observation.

`END-002` Excellent, normal and difficult sessions receive truthful distinct copy. A difficult session counts as habit but must not claim false mastery progress.

`HAB-001` One valid activity that is genuinely attempted and finalized maintains the streak. Merely opening Training does not count.

`HAB-002` Daily and weekly goals remain separate from streak and measure their configured targets.

`HAB-003` Progress indicators represent mastery, plan completion, autonomy and meaningful review. XP, levels, currency, rankings, hint economy and general achievements are prohibited.

## Nova

`NOVA-001` Nova messages are generated from structured facts, not stored prose or invented frontend explanations.

`NOVA-002` Minimum memory supports concept, mastery, recent performance, prior attempts and hints, difficulty, date, next review, Coach Decision, source and autonomy comparisons.

`NOVA-003` Copy is concise, adult, constructive and confidence-aware. It must not claim improvement without adequate evidence.

`NOVA-004` If the Very Nice to Have is implemented, semantic states are `idle`, `thinking`, `correct`, `error`, `hint`, `explaining` and `session_complete`.

`NOVA-005` Existing local assets are reused. CSS sprite positioning, transforms and core effects provide reaction; no independent limb, eye or mouth animation is required.

`NOVA-006` Thinking appears only for real processing beyond roughly 180–250 ms. Reactions do not block navigation or add more than 150 ms artificial delay. Reduced-motion users receive state and color changes without movement.

## Home and Review

`UI-001` Home presents one Coach Decision and answers what to do, why, meaningful progress and next review. DNA cannot display a competing recommendation.

`UI-002` Review sends a detection through Training Opportunity before offering or linking training. It never equates an engine error directly with a publishable exercise.

## Observability and metrics

`OBS-001` Persist auditable domain events for opportunity decisions, scoring versions, canonicalization, shadow comparison, selection reason, Coach Decision, composition, mastery transition and review due state.

`OBS-002` Operational logs contain bounded summaries and public error categories, not one verbose entry per candidate.

`OBS-003` Minimum metrics cover active training days, completion, 48-hour return, abandonment, autonomous resolution, delayed retention, recovery after failure, later recurrence when viable, rejection reasons, duplicates prevented and legacy/v2 comparisons.

## Migration, backfill and compatibility

`MIG-001` Migrations are non-destructive, idempotent, MariaDB-compatible and safe for shared hosting.

`MIG-002` Backfill is bounded, restartable, observable and reuses stored analysis. It does not reanalyze the entire history with Stockfish.

`MIG-003` Historical exercises remain available to legacy mode and auditable after canonicalization. Existing results are never rewritten as evidence that cannot be inferred.

`REG-001` Legacy Training, Flash, Scenario, Review, Analysis Board, Player DNA, historical attempts, goals and existing data continue to work in shadow mode.

## Non-goals

- LLM, external AI API or free conversation with Nova.
- XP, levels, coins, rankings, leagues, hint economy or general achievements.
- Large taxonomy, embeddings or advanced similarity.
- Stockfish rewrite or general engine optimization.
- Enterprise analytics or complex adaptive engine.
- Strong transfer claims without sufficient sample.

## Release acceptance

Version 1.6.0 is acceptable when all Must Have requirements have traceable implementation and tests; Selection v2 runs in shadow by default without changing legacy UX; backfill can pause and resume safely; the end-to-end loop produces structured reasons and learning evidence; regressions pass; and documentation states honestly whether Nova reactive visuals were delivered or deferred.
